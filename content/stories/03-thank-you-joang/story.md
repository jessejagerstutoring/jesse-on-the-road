![Waiting at Don Meung, and typing this.](placeholder-16x9)

The last day in Bangkok had finally arrived. I could have tacked on a few more days, but then I would never have seen any more of tropical Thailand.

As I type this I am waiting at Don Meung airport (almost certainly spelled wrong). It's the slightly smaller of the two, but certainly no less beautiful. It looks as if it was finished about yesterday: spotless and brand new.

It was a little hectic getting here, though. I had made the brilliant plan of booking a hotel close to the public transport to the airport. Only that was for the other airport. So I had to work out how on earth to end up in the right place.

And then Google Maps gives you a small heart attack by saying the trip takes about two hours, when I thought it would take around 20 minutes. Luckily Google Maps was completely wrong, and I got there in about an hour.

***

On the last train to the airport I kept checking whether I would get off at the right station. I think the man next to me noticed. Because at some point, he offered to walk with me to the right place at the airport.

Call me spoiled, but I always have a little suspicion in me at moments like that. Nothing could have been further from the truth.

He brought me to the check-in desk, took me to a place to eat, chatted a bit, showed me where to go through customs, and even paid for the food.

> It sounded too good to be true, but I made it through customs without any drugs.

So Joang, if you ever read this: thank you so much for your hospitality.

![The kind of airport meal someone else paid for.](placeholder-3x2)

I don't know if it's just me, but I have to say everything is so much easier here. Everyone is helpful, everything runs on time, and all the food, even at the airport, is cheap. A bottle of water costs 40 cents. Try buying that at Schiphol. In one word: great!

I'm very curious how the days in Chiang Mai, the next stop, will go. A little colder, a little less hectic, but hopefully still plenty of nice people to meet.

::: Field notes
- Bangkok has two airports. Check which one you are flying from before you book a hotel near the train.
- Don't panic at the first Google Maps estimate. Mine said two hours and it took one.
- A bottle of water at the airport costs about 40 cents.
- People may genuinely want to help you. Let them.
